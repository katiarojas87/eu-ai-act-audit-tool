# Annex III — High-Risk AI Use Cases (Regulation 2024/1689)

An AI system is **high-risk** if it falls under one of these 8 areas. Compliance
deadline for stand-alone Annex III systems: **2 December 2027** (deferred by the
Digital Omnibus from the original 2 August 2026).

## 1. Biometrics
- Remote biometric identification systems
- Biometric categorisation inferring protected characteristics (race, political opinion, religious belief, sexual orientation)
- Emotion recognition
- Exceptions: biometric verification (confirming a person is who they claim), fraud detection

## 2. Critical infrastructure
- AI as a safety component in the management/operation of critical digital infrastructure, road traffic, or supply of water, gas, heating, electricity

## 3. Education and vocational training
- Determining access or admission to educational institutions
- Evaluating learning outcomes / steering the learning process
- Assessing the appropriate level of education for a person
- Monitoring and detecting prohibited behaviour during tests

## 4. Employment, workers management and access to self-employment
- Recruitment or selection (CV screening, filtering applications, evaluating candidates)
- Decisions on promotion, termination, task allocation
- Monitoring and evaluating performance and behaviour of workers

## 5. Access to and enjoyment of essential private and public services
- Evaluating eligibility for public assistance benefits and services
- **Creditworthiness / credit scoring** of natural persons (exception: fraud detection)
- Risk assessment and pricing in life and health insurance
- Emergency call dispatching / triage of emergency services

## 6. Law enforcement
- Assessing risk of a person becoming a victim or offender
- Polygraphs and similar tools
- Evaluating reliability of evidence
- Profiling in the course of detection, investigation, prosecution

## 7. Migration, asylum and border control
- Polygraphs and similar tools
- Risk assessment of irregular migration / security / health risks
- Examination of applications for asylum, visa, residence permits
- Detection, recognition, identification of persons (except document verification)

## 8. Administration of justice and democratic processes
- Assisting a judicial authority in researching/interpreting facts and law
- Influencing the outcome of elections or referenda or voting behaviour

# Annex I — High-Risk AI in Regulated Products

AI used as a **safety component of a product** already covered by EU harmonisation
legislation, where that product requires third-party conformity assessment.
Compliance deadline: **2 August 2028**.

Examples: medical devices, in-vitro diagnostics, machinery, vehicles, aviation,
marine equipment, lifts, radio equipment, toys, pressure equipment.
