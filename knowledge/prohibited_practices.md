# Prohibited AI Practices — Article 5 (banned since 2 February 2025)

These AI practices are **forbidden** in the EU. If a system matches any of them it
is tier = PROHIBITED. Action: cease use immediately. Highest fines apply:
**up to €35,000,000 or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher.

## 1. Subliminal, manipulative or deceptive techniques
AI that deploys subliminal techniques beyond a person's consciousness, or purposefully
manipulative/deceptive techniques, that materially distort behaviour and impair
informed decision-making, causing (or likely to cause) significant harm.
Example: a system that uses imperceptible audio cues to push users toward harmful choices.

## 2. Exploitation of vulnerabilities
AI that exploits vulnerabilities of a person or group due to age, disability, or a
specific social or economic situation, materially distorting behaviour and causing
(or likely to cause) significant harm.
Example: a toy using voice assistance that encourages dangerous behaviour in children.

## 3. Social scoring
AI for evaluation or classification of people over time based on social behaviour or
personal characteristics, where the social score leads to:
(a) detrimental treatment in social contexts unrelated to where the data was generated, or
(b) detrimental treatment that is unjustified or disproportionate to the behaviour.
Example: a public-authority system scoring citizens' trustworthiness across unrelated services.

## 4. Individual criminal-risk prediction (predictive policing on profiling alone)
AI to assess or predict the risk of a person committing a criminal offence based
**solely** on profiling or personality traits.
Exception: systems that support human assessment based on objective, verifiable facts
directly linked to a criminal activity are not banned.

## 5. Untargeted facial-image scraping
AI that creates or expands facial-recognition databases through untargeted scraping
of facial images from the internet or CCTV footage.

## 6. Emotion recognition in workplace and education
AI to infer emotions of a person in the areas of workplace and education institutions.
Exception: for medical or safety reasons (e.g. fatigue detection for safety).

## 7. Biometric categorisation inferring sensitive attributes
AI that categorises people based on biometric data to deduce or infer race, political
opinions, trade-union membership, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex life or sexual
orientation.
Exception: lawful labelling/filtering of lawfully acquired biometric datasets, or
categorisation by law enforcement.

## 8. Real-time remote biometric identification (RBI) in public spaces for law enforcement
Use of "real-time" remote biometric identification systems in publicly accessible
spaces for the purposes of law enforcement.
Narrow exceptions (subject to authorisation): targeted search for specific victims
(abduction, trafficking, sexual exploitation), prevention of a specific & imminent
terrorist threat, or localisation/identification of a suspect of serious crimes.

---
**Classification note:** "significant harm", "materially distort", and the
exceptions are fact-specific. When a system is close to one of these but the harm,
intent, or exception is unclear, classify as PROHIBITED with low confidence and flag
for legal review rather than silently downgrading.
