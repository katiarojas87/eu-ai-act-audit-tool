# Obligations by Risk Tier

## PROHIBITED (banned since 2 February 2025)
- Subliminal / manipulative or deceptive techniques causing significant harm
- Exploiting vulnerabilities (age, disability, social/economic situation)
- Social scoring leading to detrimental treatment
- Predictive policing based solely on profiling
- Untargeted scraping of facial images to build recognition databases
- Emotion recognition in workplace and education (save for medical/safety)
- Biometric categorisation inferring sensitive attributes
- Real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces for law enforcement (narrow exceptions)
→ **Action: cease use immediately. Fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover.**

## HIGH-RISK (Annex I or Annex III) — 13 obligations
1. Risk management system (continuous, across lifecycle)
2. Data governance — training/validation/test data relevant, representative, error-free as far as possible, bias-examined
3. Technical documentation (Annex IV) before placing on market
4. Automatic record-keeping / logging (traceability)
5. Transparency and provision of information to deployers (clear instructions for use)
6. Human oversight (design enables effective oversight; ability to intervene/override)
7. Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity
8. Quality management system (providers)
9. Conformity assessment + EU declaration of conformity + CE marking
10. Registration in the EU database (for Annex III systems)
11. Post-market monitoring system
12. Reporting of serious incidents to authorities
13. Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) — for public bodies and providers of essential private services (banking, insurance), Annex III deployers
→ **Deadlines: Annex III = 2 December 2027; Annex I = 2 August 2028. Fines up to €15M or 3% turnover.**

## LIMITED RISK — transparency only (applies 2 August 2026)
- AI systems interacting with humans (chatbots) must disclose they are AI
- Generative AI / synthetic content (image, audio, video, text) must be machine-readable & marked as artificially generated
- Deep fakes must be labelled
- Emotion recognition / biometric categorisation systems must inform exposed persons
→ **Action: add disclosure & labelling before 2 August 2026.**

## MINIMAL RISK
- No mandatory obligations (e.g. spam filters, AI in video games, inventory optimisation)
- Voluntary codes of conduct encouraged

## GPAI — General Purpose AI models (applies 2 August 2025)
If the organisation builds on / fine-tunes / integrates a foundation model:
- Technical documentation of the model
- Information & documentation to downstream providers
- Copyright compliance policy
- Public summary of training content
- Systemic-risk models (>10^25 FLOPs): model evaluation, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity
