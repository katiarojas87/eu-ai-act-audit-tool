# Annex I — High-Risk AI in Regulated Products (full list)

An AI system is **high-risk under Annex I** when BOTH conditions hold:
1. It is intended to be used as a **safety component** of a product, OR is itself a
   product, covered by the Union harmonisation legislation listed below; AND
2. That product is required to undergo a **third-party conformity assessment** before
   being placed on the market under that legislation.

Tier = ANNEX_I. Compliance deadline: **2 August 2028** (later than Annex III's
2 December 2027, reflecting alignment with existing product-safety cycles).

## Section A — New Legislative Framework (NLF) product legislation
1. **Machinery** — Directive 2006/42/EC (Regulation (EU) 2023/1230)
2. **Toys** — Directive 2009/48/EC
3. **Recreational craft and personal watercraft** — Directive 2013/53/EU
4. **Lifts** and safety components for lifts — Directive 2014/33/EU
5. **Equipment in potentially explosive atmospheres (ATEX)** — Directive 2014/34/EU
6. **Radio equipment** — Directive 2014/53/EU
7. **Pressure equipment** — Directive 2014/68/EU
8. **Cableway installations** — Regulation (EU) 2016/424
9. **Personal protective equipment (PPE)** — Regulation (EU) 2016/425
10. **Appliances burning gaseous fuels** — Regulation (EU) 2016/426
11. **Medical devices** — Regulation (EU) 2017/745
12. **In vitro diagnostic medical devices** — Regulation (EU) 2017/746

## Section B — Transport & other sector legislation
13. **Civil aviation security** — Regulation (EC) No 300/2008
14. **Agricultural and forestry vehicles** — Regulation (EU) No 167/2013
15. **Two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles** — Regulation (EU) No 168/2013
16. **Marine equipment** — Directive 2014/90/EU
17. **Rail system interoperability** — Directive (EU) 2016/797
18. **Motor vehicles and trailers** — Regulation (EU) 2018/858
19. **Civil aviation (EASA)** — Regulation (EU) 2018/1139
20. **Unmanned aircraft / drones** — Regulation (EU) 2019/2144

---
**Classification notes:**
- "Safety component" = a component whose failure endangers health/safety of persons
  or property. AI that merely runs *on* such a product but is not safety-relevant may
  not be Annex I.
- Annex I high-risk systems carry the **same 13 obligations** as Annex III high-risk
  systems (see obligations.md), but conformity assessment is typically integrated into
  the existing product CE-marking route rather than a stand-alone AI assessment.
- If a product is covered by the legislation above but does **not** require third-party
  conformity assessment, the AI may fall out of Annex I — check the specific sectoral act.
